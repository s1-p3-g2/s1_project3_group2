## PR deadline

The course deadline is the outer limit: PR open by **2:00 AM Thursday**, reviewed and merged by
**10:00 AM Thursday**. Our own deadline is earlier:

- **Our PRs are open by:** Wednesday 8:00 PM
- **Why that time works for us:** Members of our team have a tough time reviewing PRs Thursdays. They need to have a ~2–4 hour window Wednesday to review such that everyone is on the same page for the meeting.

## Reviews

- **A requested reviewer responds within:** 12 hours
- **A PR that needs changes is updated within:** 12 hours

## Communication

- **Team channel:** Slack
- **How someone says they are out** (so the deputy can merge): A post in the slack channel with `@channel`
